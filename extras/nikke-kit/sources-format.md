Group by category, then language (ko / en / ja / zh). One row per link:

| Name | URL | Lang | Contents | Coverage | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <site> | <url> | ko | character profiles, quotes | all characters | ✅ / ⚠️ |

Categories:
1. Official sites & channels (per region: site, community, X, YouTube)
2. Fan wikis / character pages (profiles, quotes, art)
3. Story scripts (main, event, side, bond; transcript readers, playthrough playlists)
4. Voice lines (per voice language)
5. Images / Live2D-Spine (official art, sprite viewers, community model viewers: note deep-link support, model id scheme, Spine version)
6. Gaps
7. Copyright & risk notice
